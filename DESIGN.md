# ZHONGXINWOOD — design direction

An editorial material story for an owner-operated Okoume sawmill in Gabon. The product is the main visual subject: parallel board lines, warm wood, a strong typographic headline, and a useful engineering dimension study.

## Visual language
- Charcoal #171914; warm paper #f2f0e8; red end-paint accent #b54b31.
- Barlow Condensed for the large OKOUME word. DM Sans for interface and body. Libre Caslon Display for short expressive lines.
- Generous page margins, large photographs, sharp corners, fine rules, asymmetric compositions. Body text at least 16 px, ordinary controls at least 14 px, smaller secondary metadata.
- One immersive dark opening, light material/factory story, brick-red enquiry section, quiet dark close.

## Motion
- Hero image translates subtly on scroll. Heading and copy remain immediately legible.
- Dimension diagram moves from thickness to width to length as the section scrolls; buttons provide a direct keyboard/touch alternative.
- Images and headings reveal gently once. No loading sequence or scroll hijacking.
- Respect reduced motion: no parallax, no pinning, no reveal delay.

## Content and assets
- Confirmed: ZHONGXINWOOD owns its Gabon sawmill, makes Okoume sawn timber, species Aucoumea klaineana, different dimensions.
- Hero is generated material artwork, visibly labelled. Factory/product imagery is the supplied real photograph. Detail crops are the same photograph.
- No invented stock sizes, grades, drying methods, certifications, capacity, customers, or contact details.
- Form stages a request for copying/download only. It does not send, store or claim delivery of an enquiry. Contact integration remains a later step.
- English first. French may be added after translation; no inactive language toggle.
